Logs, environment/dependency identities, documentation inventory and both five-target builds are retained outside the repository under the operator's `Downloads/ComputerPilot-v0.9.0-Release-2026-10-29` directory. The production connector was already offline (`tunnel_client_not_seen`) when inspected; local release checks do not claim a live 0.9.0 rollout, and operator settings remain unchanged.
